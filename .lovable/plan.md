
# Ariba Irfan — Personal Brand Website

## Design Philosophy
A **futuristic, gradient-rich** design with dark backgrounds, glowing accents, and glass-morphism effects. High-engagement animations, scroll-triggered reveals, and micro-interactions throughout. The psychology-driven approach includes:
- **Authority triggers**: Bold headlines, powerful imagery, social proof
- **Curiosity hooks**: Interactive elements that reward exploration
- **Emotional storytelling**: Your journey from UPSC to AI entrepreneurship as a transformation narrative
- **Clear CTAs**: Easy path to book meetings and collaborate
